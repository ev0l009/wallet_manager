I am storing money as whole numbers (cents) to avoid the rounding errors that happen with decimals (floats).
Here is how it works:
• Saving money: The user enters an amount with up to 2 decimal places. The system multiplies this number by 100 and saves it as an integer (a whole number).
• Showing money: To show the actual amount again, the system just divides that whole number by 100.
